Getting aboard the Quagmire

A considerably shorter launch procedure.

The original ship loaded while you played.

In the ClickToons era, the opening story kept you entertained while the game arrived. Effects and songs followed during play, and the plug-in kept downloaded files on your computer. An ambitious voyage for a dial-up connection!

Boarding today

This demo runs directly in your browser. Choose a deck and press Play; its picture and sound load before that deck starts. No plug-in or account is required.

Sound starts after Play. You can turn effects or music off using the game’s switches. Reading the story first is entirely optional.

If loading takes a moment

Wait for the Play button to become available. If a deck fails to load, return to the level menu and try again. Refreshing returns you to the menu; Continue can restore the last saved deck boundary.
