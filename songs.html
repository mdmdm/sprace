Captain Stubble’s Songs

Hits from several decades. Taste from another planet.

Astronaut. Culinary chef. Musical phenomenon.

Harry’s old website gave him an improbable career stretching from radio to rock, disco and Broadway. Even a man stranded among hostile plants must make time for show business.

Below are the original recordings that survive in this release. Press a player to listen. These short music loops repeat until paused; nothing starts automatically.

Harry’s five advertised hits

I’m in Love with the Spore from Planet MZ
A 1960s outing with Harry’s fictional rock band. The surviving editor lists a shorter title, Spore from planet M-Z; listen below.
Ageless Motion
The 1970s progressive-rock incarnation, credited to Perhaps.
The Electric Harry
Harry’s disco-era partnership, released in 1978.
Home Away from Home
A Broadway number from his fantastically overdressed stage career.
Harry’s Theme
A theme from his fictional 1940s radio adventures.

The original page’s musical biography is part of the joke. Not every advertised recording has been recovered.
