WELCOME ABOARD THE S.S.S. QUAGMIRE

Welcome to Space Race!

Captain Stubble’s ship has a spore problem. Fortunately, our intrepid astronaut has a weed-killer problem-solving strategy.

Join Harry aboard the S.S.S. Quagmire for a little running, a little jumping and some deeply questionable encounters with alien plant life. His ship’s security system is very impressive. It would be even more impressive if it weren’t locking him in.
